Taco Labs Express is the quick side of Taco Labs. Same high-quality tacos, same margaritas, in a smaller, faster shop in downtown Boca Raton. Order at the counter, grab a seat or take it to go. No long waits, no fuss, and the prices reflect that.

Made from scratch, every day

Everything is made fresh daily. Nothing comes out of a can. Our meats are high quality, most of our ingredients are organic, we use as little seed oil as possible and no palm oil at all. Good ingredients, cooked well, that leave you feeling good after lunch.

Tacos from everywhere

We call it a lab because we experiment. Twenty tacos pull flavors from around the world: birria with a 24-hour chile consommé, al pastor chicken with pineapple and salsa verde, Korean BBQ steak, mango habanero chicken, New Delhi tandoori, smash beef on a crispy cheese crust and bang bang shrimp. Every taco is priced by tier: chicken $4.49, veggie $3.99, beef & seafood $4.99. Mix any three with chips & salsa for $14.99.

Two ways to Taco Labs

Taco Labs Express, Boca is built for quick bites and grab-and-go: tacos, margaritas and easy parking right out front.
