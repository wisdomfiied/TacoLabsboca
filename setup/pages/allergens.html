Tortillas

Every taco comes on a flour tortilla. Ask for corn at no charge.

Gluten-friendly (GF*)

Items marked GF* are gluten-friendly when made on a corn tortilla. Korean BBQ items (soy sauce), breaded and battered items (such as Chicken Fil A and Crispy Fish) contain gluten.

Vegetarian (V)

Vegetarian items contain dairy. Our vegetarian tacos are Bean, Jackfruit Birria and Korean BBQ Tofu.

Cross-contact

We share cooking surfaces and fryers, so we cannot guarantee any item is free of gluten or other allergens. Please tell us about allergies before ordering.

Hold any topping

Every item lets you leave off its toppings (no onion, no cilantro, no cheese and so on) when you order online or at the counter.
